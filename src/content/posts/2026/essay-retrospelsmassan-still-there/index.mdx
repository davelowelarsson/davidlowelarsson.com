Retrospelsmässan, the retro gaming fair in Göteborg, has its event every spring,
this year on 30 May (or maybe it was during the summer, I'm not sure anymore).
Anyway, it made me check in on the website and just look around... and there
it was. My blue design, the very telling menu and header with the pixel
characters, and my nice little MegaMan standing in the sidebar 😁.

## TL;DR

- The Retrospelsmässan site I built around 2010 to 2012 is still in use, design
  and all.
- Even the images are from my day, including a volunteer photo of a friend.
- MegaMan is still standing in the sidebar... but he doesn't rock anymore.

I was happy to see that my design is still there, and a little proud too, I
think. Most things I have built on the web are not live anymore... so it was
nice to see that this one still is.

## Back in 2010

It must have been around 2010, 2011 or 2012, I'm honestly not sure 🤔. I had
already made a [trailer for the fair in 2011](/posts/portfolio-retrospelsmassan-8-bit-ftw/),
and somewhere around then I built the website too. The whole WordPress theme
and the backend, I set it up and maintained it for years. I don't maintain it
anymore, but it's still in use.

There's a snapshot on the Wayback Machine from 2014, and it looks almost exactly
like today. The blue design and the header are still there, and the menu looks
just like when I built it. The footer still says "© 2010-2015" 😅.

see how the menu and the images are still "screen width aware"... just the same
way as when I built it. And even the images are from my day.

Clicking around a bit more, I found the page asking for volunteers, and the
photo on it is of a friend. It was taken maybe ten years ago, and it's still
there, with the same text offering free food and drink for helping out.

That is pretty much how we ran the whole thing. We all worked for free, nobody
had a paycheck, it was only nostalgia and love for old games that made us do
it... and it was hard and a lot of work.

</MediaAside>

The website was my part of that, and seeing it still there reminded me of my
uncle.

## Pointing at buildings

He worked as a kalkylator and architect at
F O Peterson & Söner for decades, from around the late 70s, and he could drive
around the city and point at buildings he had built or been involved with.

When working digitally that is so much rarer. Everything gets rebuilt with such
speed that it's hardly ever seen... most of the webpages and things I have
built are not maintained or live anymore, or they have been rebuilt with some
new and cooler framework. And with the internet and AI running faster and
faster, I don't think that will slow down. So it's nice to have at least one I
can still point at, even if it's a small pixel MegaMan in a sidebar.

## Why isn't MegaMan rocking?

Back then there was a newsletter field in the sidebar, the small one that said
"Nyhetsbrev ..." with an Ok button, and when someone clicked into it MegaMan
started rocking. When the field lost focus he went back to standing still. It
was such a small thing, a little GIF and a few lines of jQuery, but I really
liked it.

This time I didn't remember how to trigger it. So I went digging in the page
source and found him, an empty div with the class `megaman_still_image`, and my
old jQuery file still being served, swapping the class whenever a field with
the id `mce-EMAIL` got focus. But that field is gone. The newsletter was
removed at some point, so there is nothing left to click into, and MegaMan just
stands there. A bit sad, honestly.

So I rebuilt the field here, just as it was, with the original still image and
the rocking GIF. Nothing is sent anywhere... he just rocks while the field has
focus 🎸.
