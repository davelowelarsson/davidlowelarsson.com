Retrospelsmässan, the retro gaming fair in Göteborg, was held on 30 May this
year, so around then I checked the website. And there it was... my blue design,
the header with the pixel characters, the menu, and MegaMan still standing in
the sidebar. I was happy to see it 😁.

## TL;DR

- The Retrospelsmässan site I built around 2010 to 2012 is still in use, and
  MegaMan is still standing in the sidebar.
- The newsletter signup where he used to rock is gone, so I rebuilt the field
  as a small demo.
- Most of my other web work from that era is gone or rebuilt, this one stayed.

## The newsletter field

So the sidebar used to have a newsletter signup, a small email field with an Ok
button. When the field got focus MegaMan started to rock, a little dance GIF,
and when it lost focus he went back to standing still. That was the whole
trick. The newsletter is gone now, so he just stands there.

The field below is a recreation, nothing is sent anywhere, but he rocks again
while it has focus.

## Still in use

I built the whole thing around 2010 to 2012, the WordPress theme and the
backend, and I set it up and maintained it. It's still in use, which I think is
kind of wild. The 2014 snapshot on the Wayback Machine looks nearly identical
to today, and the footer still says "© 2010-2015" 😅. The site is more of an
information site now, but the menu and the images still adapt to the screen
width.

Most of my other web work from that era is gone or has been rebuilt. Things
move fast, and AI and the internet move even faster... but this one stayed. I
made a free [trailer for the fair in 2011](/posts/portfolio-retrospelsmassan-8-bit-ftw/)
and wrote about coming back to it in
[In praise of boring tools](/posts/essay-in-praise-of-boring-tools/).

## The volunteers

The photo is roughly ten years old and shows one of the volunteers, a
Funktionär. For me it says how the event was run. All volunteers, nobody getting
paid, a lot of nostalgia and love for old games, and a lot of hard work.

It also made me think of my uncle, who worked for decades, from around the late
70s, as a kalkylator (cost estimator) and architect at F O Peterson & Söner.
Some people get to point at durable things they made. This little site is my
small version of that.

So we'll see how long MegaMan keeps standing there... time will tell.
